🎯 Goal

First team to reach 101 points wins. Play multiple rounds.

📊 Scoring

When a player empties their hand, their team gets the sum of all opponent hand pips.

🎲 Both Ends Open

Both ends of the board are always playable — including the opening tile. Play on either end you match. Doubles are placed crossways but don't lock a side.

🔄 Turns

Match board ends, draw if stuck, pass if boneyard empty. 75s timer.
